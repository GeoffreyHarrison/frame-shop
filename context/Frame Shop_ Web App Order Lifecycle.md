The following steps outline the lifecycle of a custom framing order within the system, from initial entry to customer pickup, categorized by functional areas to assist in process mapping.

# Phase 1: Order Creation and Documentation

* **Customer Identification:** The process begins in the **Customer Database**. Staff search for an existing customer or create a new entry (recording name, phone, email, and tax status).  
* **Order Entry:** Details are entered into the **Order Entry Screen**, including the order number (from the POS system), due date, frame SKU (molding), frame size, mat colors, glass type, and mounting instructions.  
* **Ticket Generation:** Upon submission, the system generates an **Order Ticket**. This is a formatted physical document that is printed and travels with the artwork through the store to guide production.

# Phase 2: Material Procurement and Inventory Management

* **Molding Procurement:** Frame SKUs are automatically sent to an **Order List** based on vendor codes. Staff check the shop's stock; if unavailable, they hit "Order" to move the item to a vendor-specific list (e.g., Larson or Decor) for purchasing.  
* **Mat Procurement:** All mats are sent to the **Mat List**. Staff review the shop's bins for leftovers or stock; if found, they mark it "Stock" or "Got" to remove it from the list. If not, they select a distributor to add it to a purchasing list.  
* **Glass Review:** Incomplete orders appear on the **Glass Review** page. Staff use this list to calculate how many boxes of specific glass types (like Museum or AR glass) need to be ordered from their single vendor.  
* **Status Tracking:** The system tracks three specific material milestones: **Put on List**, **Ordered**, and **Received**.

# Phase 3: Production Milestones

* **Verification (The "Checked" Milestone):** Before building, orders appear on the **"Orders Not Verified"** list. A verifier remeasures the artwork and enters a **Verified Size** (the exact interior frame/glass size) to ensure accuracy. The order is then marked "Verified".  
* **Frame Building:** Once an order is "Verified" and the molding is marked "Received," it populates the **"Frames to Build"** list. After the frame is constructed at the warehouse, it is marked **"Built"**.  
* **Internal Preparation ("On Table"):** While the frame is being built, another staff member cuts mats and mounts the artwork. When these internal components are ready, the order is marked **"On Table"** (or "Table").  
* **Fitting:** When both the "Built" and "On Table" milestones are met, the order is ready for the final assembly stage, where glass is cut to the verified size and the piece is fitted and finished.

# Phase 4: Completion and Customer Pickup

* **Marking Complete:** Once fitting is finished, the order is marked **"Complete"**.  
* **Storage and Notification:** Staff quality-check and wrap the piece, then assign it a physical storage location (e.g., **"Bin B"**). They contact the customer and record the notification method (e.g., "texted") in the system.  
* **Pickup:** When the customer arrives, staff use the **"Pickups"** menu (historically "EPS Pickups") or search the customer name to locate the bin.  
* **Final Milestone:** The order is marked **"Picked Up"**, which removes it from the active tracking queue while archiving it in the **Order History**.

# Management and Exception Tools

* **Comments/Updates:** Staff can add notes to the **Order Details Page** to track disruptions, such as shipping delays or design changes.  
* **Flags:** Orders can be tagged with a **"Delayed"** flag, which moves them to a specific **Backorder Report** for management monitoring.  
* **Reorders:** If a material is damaged during production, a **"Reorder"** button places that specific item back onto the procurement list.
